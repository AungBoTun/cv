Voice & AI Data Pipeline

Ingest calls, transcribe, redact, summarize, and answer queries with optional geo context.

Benefits

  • Searchable knowledge from calls
  • PII redaction and auditability
  • Geo-aware answers (PostGIS optional)

Quick Steps

  1. Deploy ingest API; set STT/LLM API keys
  2. Store audio in S3/GCS; transcripts in Postgres
  3. Use Grafana/Tempo to inspect traces
